Vitality & Prevention

Daily Wellness & Preventive Health

Accessible to all guests without login. Evidence-based lifestyle and wellness tips.

🛡️ Medical Safety & Disclaimer Notice:

Our Guest Wellness Chatbot provides preventative lifestyle guidelines only. It does not prescribe medications, pharmaceutical drugs, or diagnostic treatments. For clinical care, please log in as a Patient.

🌙

Sleep Hygiene

  • Consistent 7-9 hours sleep routine
  • Disconnect screens 45 min prior
  • Maintain dark, cool room temperature
💧

Hydration Balance

  • 2.5 to 3 liters clean water daily
  • Pale yellow urine hydration check
  • Drink warm water upon waking
💺

Ergonomics & Posture

  • Monitor positioned at eye level
  • 20-20-20 screen relief rule
  • Lumbar spine support cushion
🧘

Stress Management

  • 4-7-8 deep diaphragmatic breathing
  • 15-minute nature walks
  • Mindful evening wind-down
